# Source guide per field

General principle: prefer official registries and the company's own materials; use aggregators to fill gaps and cross-check. Record the URL of everything you use — the final report to the user must cite sources.

## Portugal (primary focus)

| Field | Sources (in order of preference) |
|---|---|
| Legal name, NIF, legal form, HQ address, incorporation date | **publicacoes.mj.pt** (free official gazette of company acts — search by NIF or name), **Racius** (racius.com — free profile pages indexed by Google), Portal da Empresa / registo comercial, **NIF.pt** |
| Website URL, logo | Company site (search `<name> <city>`), Google, LinkedIn company page |
| Business description, portfolio | Company website ("About", "Serviços", "Produtos"), LinkedIn company page, Google Maps listing |
| Photos | Company website / social media — usable for internal mockups only; flag licensing for anything published |
| Management / directors | Free tiers of **Racius** and **eInforma** (einforma.pt), MJ publications (appointments appear in registered acts) |
| Employee count | LinkedIn headcount range; Racius sometimes shows bands |
| Shareholders | **MJ publications**: for Lda companies, founding shareholders and quota transfers appear in the free gazette (search the company's act history). SA share registers are not public — note that. Racius/eInforma free tiers occasionally list partners. |
| Financials | Racius and eInforma free tiers sometimes show headline revenue/result bands or single-year figures. Full IES accounts are paid (Informa D&B, SABI, portal da justiça certidões). Larger companies: news articles, sector rankings (e.g. "1000 Maiores"), annual reports if SA. |

Practical tips:
- Racius profile URLs follow `racius.com/<company-slug>/` and are well indexed — a site-restricted search usually lands directly on the profile.
- publicacoes.mj.pt search works best with the NIF (NIPC). Acts are in Portuguese legalese; look for "constituição de sociedade" (incorporation — lists founding quotas), "designação de membro(s) de órgão social" (director appointments), "transmissão de quotas".
- Cross-check the NIF: Portuguese company NIFs (NIPC) start with 5 (or 9 for some entities) and are 9 digits.

## Other countries

Same field structure; swap the registry layer:

- **UK**: Companies House (free: officers, PSC/shareholder control, full filed accounts as PDFs — financials are actually free here)
- **Spain**: BORME (free gazette, like MJ publications), einforma.es, Libreborme
- **France**: Pappers.fr (free: officers, shareholders when filed, financial statements), data.inpi.fr, societe.com
- **Germany**: Handelsregister/registerbekanntmachungen (free announcements), Unternehmensregister & Bundesanzeiger (free published accounts for many GmbHs), North Data (free tier)
- **Netherlands**: KVK extracts are paid; company websites + jaarverslagen for larger firms
- **Belgium**: KBO/BCE public search (free registry data), NBB Balance Sheet Central (free filed accounts)
- **US**: state Secretary-of-State registries (registration, agents; no financials for private cos), SEC EDGAR for anything public
- **Anywhere**: OpenCorporates for registry basics; LinkedIn for description/headcount; company website for everything else; local official gazette for acts

If public companies come up: use the latest annual report / investor-relations page — that's the highest-quality free financial source there is.
